### Game Overview
Cell to Singularity is an idle evolution clicker game. The player starts as a single cell and evolves life, builds civilizations, and progresses toward technological singularity. The game combines science facts with idle gameplay, tapping, generators, upgrades, and prestige systems.

### Core Gameplay
- Tap to generate currency.
- Buy and upgrade Generators (buildings) that produce currency passively.
- Unlock and progress through massive Tech Trees.
- Perform Reboots (prestige) to gain permanent upgrades.

### Main Currencies
- Entropy: Used in early Life stage.
- Idea: Used in Civilization stage.
- Darwinium: Premium currency (real money or earned).
- Metabits: Gained from Entropy on Reboot (prestige).
- Knowledge: Gained from Idea on Reboot (prestige).
- Fossils: Mesozoic Valley currency.
- Stardust: Beyond (space) simulation currency.

### Main Simulations / Areas
1. Primary Simulation
   - Tech Tree of Life (cell → human)
   - Dawn of Civilization (stone age → future)
   
2. Mesozoic Valley (Dinosaurs)
   - Unlocked after Flatworm.
   - Uses Fossils and Mutagen.
   - Has its own prestige (extinction events).

3. Beyond (Space)
   - Unlocked later via Research Levels.
   - Uses Stardust.

4. Reality Engine
   - Prestige system where you spend Metabits and Knowledge for permanent boosts.

### Progression Flow
- Start tapping for Entropy → buy Amino Acids, DNA, cells, animals, etc.
- Reach Humans → unlock Ideas and Civilization tree.
- Reach key milestones (Flatworm, Singularity) → Reboot for Metabits/Knowledge.
- Unlock Mesozoic Valley and Beyond for extra boosts to the main simulation.
- Research Levels give permanent production multipliers.

### Important Mechanics
- Tapping is important early game.
- Generators produce currency even when offline.
- Upgrades multiply production.
- Boosters give temporary speed multipliers.
- Daily Mutations, Glitches, Achievements, and Modules exist.
- The game has educational flavor text explaining real science and history.

Key advice for players:
- Buy cheap upgrades first.
- Use "Max" purchase mode wisely.
- Reboot when progress slows down.
- Progress multiple simulations in parallel as they boost each other.

### Tech Trees Overview

**Tech Trees** are the main route to progress in Cell to Singularity. They contain all upgrades and generators in the game. Each Tech Tree has different subdivisions of history and organisms (e.g., Primordial Soup, Iron Age). 

There are three main Tech Trees:
- **Primary Simulation**: Tech Tree of Life + Dawn of Civilization
- **Mesozoic Valley** (Dinosaurs)
- **Beyond** (Space)

Plus 18 Exploration Tech Trees (limited-time events).

Each tree has timeline branches that hold upgrades and generators. The first Tech Tree (Life) unlocks after reaching 10 Entropy. Arrows point to newly available trees.

### 1. Tech Tree of Life (Primary Simulation - Entropy)
The starting tree. Focuses on biological evolution from chemicals to complex life.

**Key Habitats / Sections**:
- **Primordial Soup**: First habitat. Tap for Entropy. Early generators:
  - Amino Acid
  - DNA
  - Prokaryotic Cell
  - Eukaryotic Cell
  - Amoeba, Tardigrade, Bacteriophage

**Progression**:
- Sponge → Jellyfish → Flatworm → Fish → Tetrapod → Mammal → Ape → Human
- Unlocks more complex life (reptiles, birds) via Mesozoic Valley ranks.
- Generators produce Entropy passively after buying multiples and upgrades.

### 2. Dawn of Civilization (Primary Simulation - Idea)
Unlocks after reaching Humans and First Idea.

**Key Sections**:
- Stone Tools, Fire, Language, Agriculture
- Writing, Bronze Age, Iron Age
- Middle Ages, Renaissance, Industrial Revolution
- Information Age, Space Age → Singularity

Tapping the Brain icon produces Idea. Generators build human society and technology.

**Milestones**:
- Singularity causes crash → first Reboot (Reality Engine).

### 3. Mesozoic Valley Tech Tree
Unlocked after Flatworm (Mysterious Rock → Great Fossil Portal).

**Focus**: Dinosaur evolution from Archosaurs through extinction events.

**Key Generators** (examples):

- Higher ranks unlock reptiles and birds in Primary Simulation + give permanent multipliers.

### 4. Beyond Tech Tree (Space)
Unlocked at Research Level 10 via Black Hole Portal.

**Focus**: Solar System → Observable Universe.

**Key Generators** (examples):

**Benefits**: Strong permanent multipliers to Entropy and Idea production in Primary Simulation.

### Achievements System

**Achievements** are challenges that reward players when completed. They provide Darwinium, trivia, and a sense of progress. As of recent versions, the game has around **530 achievements** in total.

#### Main Types of Achievements:
1. **General Achievements** (~22)
   - Major story and progression milestones.
   - Examples in Primary Simulation:
     - *Beginnings*: Buy first Prokaryotic Cell.
     - *Upward and Onward*: Buy Jellyfish.
     - *Out of the Ocean*: Buy Tetrapod.
     - *If I Had A Hammer*: Buy Stone Tools.
     - *First Human*: Buy Human.
     - *Singularity Glass*: Reach Singularity (first crash).
     - *Déjà vu*: Reach Singularity again.
     - *Terraform Mars Complete*: Fully terraform Mars.
     - *King of the Jungle*: Unlock Feliforms (Research Level 46).

2. **Mesozoic Valley General Achievements**:
   - *Dawn of the Dinosaurs*: Unlock Mesozoic Valley (break Mysterious Rock 200 times).
   - *Massive Comet*: Trigger first Extinction Event.
   - *Prehistoric Research*: Unlock T-Rex (Rank 26).
   - *Regicide*: Unlock Giganotosaurus (Rank 48).

3. **Beyond General Achievements**:
   - *We Have Lift-off*: Unlock Beyond simulation.
   - *Only Eight*: Unlock all planets (up to Neptune).
   - *Interstellar Travel*: Reach Alpha Centauri.
   - *To Infinity and Beyond*: Complete the Beyond simulation (Limit of Knowledge trophy).

4. **Generator Achievements** (Majority of total):
   - **Primary Simulation**: 46 generators × 5 tiers (50 / 100 / 250 / 500 / 1000 owned) = 230 achievements.
     - Rewards: Mostly 1 Darwinium each (last tier gives 2).
   - **Mesozoic Valley**: 25 generators × 2 tiers (75 / 200 owned) = 50 achievements.
   - **Beyond**: Many rank-based achievements (Rank 5/10/8/6 etc. on celestial bodies) + constellation unlocks.

5. **Secret Achievements** (~28):
   - Hidden challenges, often fun or exploration-based.
   - Examples:
     - *Shark Week*: Unlock all sharks from Daily Rewards.
     - *Monkeying Around*: Make the Ape dab (tap repeatedly).
     - *Speed Demon*: Reach Singularity in under 6 minutes.
     - Others involve finding hidden objects (Galapagos Island, Archie’s fossil, secret dolphins, etc.) using camera/map mode.

#### Important Notes:
- Achievements are **permanent** — Reboots do not reset them.
- Tapping an achievement in the menu gives Darwinium + educational trivia.
- They are a great source of free Darwinium, especially generator count achievements.
- Progressing all simulations (Primary + Mesozoic + Beyond) and completing Explorations unlocks the most achievements.

#### 1. General / Premium
**Darwinium** (Premium Currency)

- Obtained by: Real money purchases, Objectives, Darwinium Synthesizer, Geodes, Galactic Nursery, Astronomy Missions, Codes, News giveaways, Next Level Mindset upgrades.
- Used for: Boosters, Darwinium Upgrades (permanent), Modules, Augmentations, Geodes, Mutagen, Dark Matter, etc.

- Mesozoic Valley Geodes / Traits: Stone Geode (2/ad), Gold Geode (20), Diamond Geode (100), Random Trait (2/ad)

#### 2. Primary Simulation
**Entropy**
- First currency in the game.
- Used for: Tech Tree of Life (biological evolution nodes).
- Generated by: Tapping Planetary Garden, generators (Amino Acid, DNA, cells, animals, etc.), and various gardens (Primordial Soup, Ocean, Land).
- Passive + active production increases with upgrades.

**Idea**
- Second currency in Primary Simulation.
- Used for: Dawn of Civilization Tech Tree (human society & technology).
- Generated by: Tapping the Brain (Idea Generator — unlocked after First Idea), generators (Stone Tools → Space Age), and civilization gardens.
- Passive + active production improves with upgrades.

#### 3. Reality Engine (Prestige)
**Metabit**
- Gained by converting Entropy during Reboot.
- Used for: Metabit Upgrades and other permanent boosts in Reality Engine.
- Available after unlocking Fish.

**Knowledge**
- Gained by converting Idea during Reboot.
- Used for: Knowledge Upgrades and other permanent boosts in Reality Engine.

**Research Chip**
- Earned by: Leveling up Research Level, Next Level Guidance upgrades, Objectives.
- Used to: Increase Entropy and Idea production permanently.
- Boost starts at x0.05 per chip. Every 10 chips, the boost doubles.

- Used to: Buy dinosaur generators.
- Generated by: Tapping Mysterious Rock/dinosaurs, Quantum Charge boosters, generators.
- Resets on Extinction (Asteroid) events.

**Mutagen**
- Secondary currency.
- Used to: Upgrade Trait Cards (automation + multipliers).
- Does NOT reset on extinction or prestige — carries over.
- Obtained from: Geodes, generator milestones, Darwinium purchases.
